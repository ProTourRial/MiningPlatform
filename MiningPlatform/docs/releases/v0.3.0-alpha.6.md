# MiningPlatform v0.3.0-alpha.6

**Date:** 2026-08-21
**Schema:** 12
**Latest migration:** `20260821020000_referral_fee_foundation`

This alpha establishes a controlled reconciliation-exception lifecycle and exact referral economics while preserving the no-real-funds boundary.

## Product behavior

- Standard pool fee: **0.50%** (5,000 PPM).
- Valid referral fee: **0.375%** (3,750 PPM).
- Referral commission: **0.125% of gross reward** (1,250 PPM), funded from the charged fee.
- Default code: `MP05`; beneficiary is the site-donation ledger liability.
- Worker format: `account.worker#CODE`.
- Every user receives a personal referral code; self-referral and attribution replacement are rejected.
- Auto withdrawal is user-selectable per mining account/asset and defaults to `OFF`.

## Financial controls

- Original reconciliation evidence is immutable and exceptions cannot post rewards.
- Corrected evidence requires request and decision by two different verified OWNER accounts.
- Approval creates a replacement `MATCHED` reconciliation and one settlement event; rejection remains non-posting.
- Referral allocations separately persist charged fee, beneficiary commission, and retained platform revenue.
- Auto withdrawal `ON` remains ineffective while payout gates are disabled or a verified destination is absent.

## Boundary

This remains an alpha. Real provider certification, wallet signing, transaction broadcast, conversion, real donation payout, and user payout remain disabled pending their production gates.
